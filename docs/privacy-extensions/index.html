Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22. Applies to three browser extensions: Website Contact Finder: Emails & Phones, Tech Stack of this Tab: Site Detector and Page Image Finder & Downloader.

None of the extensions collects, stores, transmits or sells any personal or usage data.

Website Contact Finder: Emails & Phones

  • When you click the toolbar icon, the extension reads the HTML of the current tab inside your browser and extracts email addresses, phone numbers and social profile links. This happens locally and the result is shown only in the popup.
  • Nothing is sent to us or to any third party. There is no analytics, telemetry, account, cookie, or remote code.
  • The extension stores nothing (no storage permission). Copying to the clipboard and CSV download happen only when you press those buttons.
  • Permissions: activeTab and scripting, used only to read the current page after you click the icon.
  • The button "Crawl the whole site or a list of sites" opens https://apify.com/pennydata/website-contact-extractor in a new tab. That site is operated by Apify and has its own privacy policy; the extension passes it no data.

Tech Stack of this Tab: Site Detector

  • When you click the toolbar icon, the extension reads the HTML, the cookie names (never cookie values) and the presence of well-known JavaScript globals (names only, never their values) of the current tab inside your browser, and matches them against signature rules bundled in the extension. This happens locally and the result is shown only in the popup.
  • Nothing is sent to us or to any third party. There is no analytics, telemetry, account, cookie, or remote code.
  • The extension stores nothing (no storage permission). Copying to the clipboard and CSV download happen only when you press those buttons.
  • Permissions: activeTab and scripting, used only to read the current page after you click the icon.
  • The button "Bulk-check a list of sites" opens https://apify.com/pennydata/website-tech-stack-detector in a new tab. That site is operated by Apify and has its own privacy policy; the extension passes it no data.

Page Image Finder & Downloader

  • When you click the toolbar icon, the extension reads the current tab's DOM inside your browser to list its images. This happens locally and the list is shown only in the popup.
  • Nothing is sent to us or to any third party. There is no analytics, telemetry, account, cookie, or remote code. The popup shows thumbnails by loading the image URLs the page itself already uses.
  • The extension stores nothing (no storage permission). Copying to the clipboard and downloading happen only when you press those buttons.
  • Permissions: activeTab and scripting read the current page after you click the icon; downloads saves the images you select to your Downloads folder.
  • The "Bulk" button opens https://apify.com/pennydata/website-image-extractor in a new tab. That site is operated by Apify and has its own privacy policy; the extension passes it no data.

Third-party data on pages

Email addresses and phone numbers shown in the popup come from the page you are viewing. They are displayed to you only, are not copied anywhere unless you press Copy or Download, and are never transmitted to us.
